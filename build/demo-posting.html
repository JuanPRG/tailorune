About the role

Contoso Retail is hiring a Senior Product Analyst to own the metrics behind our merchandising platform. You will build dashboards in SQL and Python, run experiments end to end, and partner with merchandising leads to turn findings into pricing decisions.

What you will do

  • Own the reporting pipeline the merchandising team plans against each week.
  • Design and run pricing experiments, and be accountable for what ships from them.
  • Replace manual spreadsheet forecasting with models the business can audit.
  • Shorten the reporting cycle and show the business impact of the analysis you shipped.

What we are looking for

Someone who has shortened reporting cycles before and can point at decisions that changed because of their work. Strong SQL and Python, comfort designing an experiment rather than only reading one, and the judgement to know which questions are worth answering.
